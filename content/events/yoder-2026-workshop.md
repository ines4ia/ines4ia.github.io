## Overview

**Agentes, padrões e modelagem na era da IA** (*Agents, Patterns and Modeling in the Age of AI*) is a one-day workshop with **Joseph Yoder** and **Kyle Brown**, held on **November 30, 2026**, at the **Informatics Center (CIn)** of the **Federal University of Pernambuco (UFPE)**, in Recife, Brazil. The sessions cover the design of LLM-based agentic systems grounded in Domain-Driven Design, architecture patterns for cloud applications, and a hands-on mini-workshop on domain modeling with AI assistance.

The workshop is sponsored by **INES**, **CIn/UFPE** and **SPG**.

Full details are available on the [workshop website](https://vinicius-resende-cin.github.io/workshop-JosephYoder/).
