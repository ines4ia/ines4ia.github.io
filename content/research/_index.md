## SE4AI — Software Engineering for AI

Applying rigorous software engineering principles to the development, deployment, and maintenance of AI-based systems. Themes include requirements engineering, architecture, testing, quality assurance, and more — all tailored for AI systems.

## AI4SE — AI for Software Engineering

Leveraging AI and machine learning techniques to automate and improve software engineering tasks: from code generation and review to bug prediction, traceability, and process mining.

## Transversal Themes

Cutting-across both perspectives, transversal themes such as empirical studies, open science, and human factors connect the two pillars and ensure rigorous, reproducible research.
