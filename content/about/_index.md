## What is INES.IA?

INES.IA — the Brazilian Institute for Software Engineering and Artificial Intelligence — is a National Institute for Science and Technology (INCT) funded by CNPq, the Brazilian National Council for Scientific and Technological Development.

## Mission

To promote research, development, and innovation in Software Engineering, aiming to establish major results that will be a reference for building dependable software with high productivity, quality, performance, and security; radically diminishing project failure rate, rising satisfaction in product quality, with controlled cost and benefit.

## Goals

1. Develop high international standard research in Software Engineering
2. Graduate highly qualified human resources in Software Engineering
3. Promote knowledge transfer to society through mini-courses, specialized training, lectures, workshops, and conferences
4. Promote knowledge transfer to industry by collaborating with companies to enhance competitiveness and drive innovation

## Our Network

We bring together more than 50 researchers from 25 institutions across Brazil and abroad to tackle the foundational and applied challenges posed by the intersection of software engineering and artificial intelligence.

## Research Directions

Our work bridges two strategic directions:

- **SE4AI** — applying software engineering principles, processes, and tools to the development of AI-based systems
- **AI4SE** — using artificial intelligence techniques to improve software engineering tasks and processes

## Governance

INES.IA is coordinated by a team of researchers at CIn-UFPE, with a multi-institutional steering committee representing host and associated institutions across Brazil. Meet the full team on our [Team page](/team/).
