The [Brazilian Software Congress (CBSoft 2026)](https://cbsoft.sbc.org.br/2026/), organized by the Brazilian Computer Society (SBC), took place on September 8–11 at the University of São Paulo (USP). Researchers and students from INES.IA were involved throughout the event, as paper authors, panel organizers, and track organizers.

**Track organization.** INES.IA members helped organize several CBSoft tracks, among them the Brazilian Symposium on Software Engineering (SBES), the Brazilian Symposium on Systematic and Automated Software Testing (SAST), the Tools track, and the special *SBES 40 Years* track.

**Panels.** INES.IA organized Panel 4, *Software Engineering Education*. Alessandro Garcia chaired Panel 3, *Navigating the Uncharted Implications of Advanced AI in Software Engineering*.

**Papers.** A full list is hard to compile, but here are some of the papers by INES.IA members presented at CBSoft 2026:

- **How AI Coding Agents Resolve Merge Conflicts: An Empirical Study** — Heleno de Souza Campos Junior and Leonardo Murta (SBES 2026, Research Track)
- **Design-System-Aware Development with AI: Evaluating Productivity and Design Consistency** — Luciane Silva, Thayssa Rocha, Nicole Davila, and Gustavo Pinto (SBES 2026, Industry Track)
- **A Spec-Driven Approach for Source Code Summarization of COBOL Legacy Systems with LLMs** — João Pedro Vaz, Vinicius Araújo, Murilo Souza, Michael Silva, and Rodrigo Bonifácio (SBES 2026, Insightful Ideas Track)
- **Does ISO-Grounded NFR Specification Improve LLM Code Generation?** — João Pedro Monteiro Pereira and Vinicius Cardoso Garcia (SBCARS 2026)
- **Building AI-Intensive Software with AI: Early Results and a Cautionary Tale on Measuring Development Cost** — Victor Neves, Kiev Gama, and Vinicius Cardoso Garcia (ISE 2026)
- **From Pixels to Interaction Graphs: Automated Usability Analytics in the SmartAPSUS Case Study** — Igor Vanderlei, Rodrigo Andrade, et al. (ISE 2026)
