---
title: "INES.IA Officially Launched as Brazil's National Software Engineering Institute"
date: 2025-03-15
tag: "Announcement"
summary: "CNPq formalises the new edition of INES, uniting over 50 researchers from 25 institutions under the banner of INES.IA — the Brazilian Institute for Software Engineering and Artificial Intelligence."
---

The Brazilian National Council for Scientific and Technological Development (CNPq) has officially approved the new edition of the National Institute for Software Engineering, now rebranded as **INES.IA** to reflect its expanded focus on Artificial Intelligence.

The institute brings together more than 50 researchers from 25 universities and research centres across Brazil, organised around 17 research themes covering two strategic directions: Software Engineering for AI (SE4AI) and AI for Software Engineering (AI4SE).

The launch marks the beginning of a five-year research programme with the goal of generating scientific knowledge, developing open-source tools, and transferring technology to industry and government partners.

For more information, contact us at [contact@ines.org.br](mailto:contact@ines.org.br).
