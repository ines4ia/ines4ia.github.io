The first INES.IA Annual Workshop took place in Recife, Pernambuco, hosted by the Centro de Informática da UFPE. The two-day event gathered more than 80 researchers, students, and industry partners to share early results, align research agendas, and strengthen collaboration across the institute's 17 research themes.

Highlights included keynote presentations on large language models for software engineering, panel discussions on SE4AI challenges in safety-critical systems, and a poster session showcasing ongoing PhD research across member institutions.

The workshop also featured a dedicated session with industry partners to identify technology transfer opportunities and define joint case studies for the coming year.

The next annual workshop is expected to take place in 2026.
