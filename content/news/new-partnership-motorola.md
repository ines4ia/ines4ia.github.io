---
title: "INES.IA Signs Research Partnership with Motorola Solutions"
date: 2025-08-10
tag: "Partnership"
summary: "A new formal research partnership with Motorola Solutions will fund applied research on AI-assisted software testing and quality assurance, with results expected to be published as open science."
---

INES.IA has signed a research partnership agreement with Motorola Solutions to advance applied research on AI-assisted software testing and quality assurance. The two-year collaboration will focus on practical challenges in embedded software development, with results published as open science.

The partnership is expected to produce at least two peer-reviewed publications, one open-source testing tool, and training materials for industry practitioners.

This is the first of several planned industry partnerships as INES.IA works to strengthen the bridge between academic research and software development practice in Brazil.
